# Attempt 07 · Urban Silhouette Noir

Stress-test three of Arc 01's hardest layouts as three standalone vertical 9:16 panels. Use the locked Arc 01 character sheets for silhouettes and proportions.

**Look.** Crisp, pitch-black foreground silhouettes against a modern Indonesian city at night in the rain. Use cold slate, fluorescent spill, red taillights and green exit light where motivated by the scene. Keep faces sparse, with selective pale eyes or a thin rim light.

| Shot | Setup | What must read on Pass 1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A · RHD rearview threat | Rainy Indonesian car interior from the front passenger-side angle. The driver and wheel sit on the right. A pale eye or thin rim catches the rear-seat threat in the rearview mirror. | The mirror sits ahead of the driver and faces the rear seat at a plausible angle. The reflected threat matches the seat position, and the driver fits the cabin. |
| B · Minimarket glass | A figure stands behind closed double minimarket glass doors. View from the wet sidewalk at an oblique angle under fluorescent spill. | The figure stays behind both glass leaves, with believable door-to-person scale. Glass reflections follow the angle of the doors and leave the figure readable. |
| C · Underpass exit | View down a dark, wet pedestrian underpass toward the green `PINTU KELUAR` sign above the station glass. The threat is silhouetted at the far end. | Corridor lines, pillars and wet-floor reflections agree on depth. The distant figure has plausible scale, the glass reads correctly and `PINTU KELUAR` is legible. |

**Pass metric.** Agent 03 must deliver **3 of 3 usable shots on Pass 1 with zero anatomy, scale or glass-reflection fixes**. Inspect the initial panels at phone size and record a verdict per shot. Any correction in those categories fails the first-pass target.
