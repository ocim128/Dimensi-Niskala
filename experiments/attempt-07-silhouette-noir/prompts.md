# Urban Silhouette Noir — Pass-1 prompts and review

## Method

Built-in image generation; exactly one invocation and one take for each of A, B, and C. The locked driver and teenage passenger sheets at `public/assets/characters/arc-01-driver/sheet.png` and `public/assets/characters/arc-01-passenger-teen/sheet.png` were supplied to each invocation as identity and proportion references only; their painted rendering was explicitly excluded. No retries, inpainting, anatomy fixes, geometry fixes, glass fixes, compositing, or retouching.

The original generated images were 941 × 1672 PNGs. For exact 9:16 delivery, each was mechanically center-cropped to 936 × 1664 (2 pixels left, 3 right, 4 top and bottom) and encoded as lossless WebP. This is a uniform format/aspect conversion, not a correction to depicted content. The contact sheet shows all three at 360 × 640 pixels per panel, with labels above.

## Pass-1 review

Reviewed the original generated images and the 360 × 640-per-panel contact sheet. These are the agent's observations, not owner approval. The pass requires all three shots to be usable without anatomy, scale or reflection corrections.

- **A — Pass, with a caveat.** The driver and wheel read on the right, the driver fits the cabin, and the hanging mirror shows a rear-seat figure with visible pale eyes. The mirror is large and the reflected seat position is suggested rather than fully verifiable, but no definite geometry or anatomy break is apparent at phone size.
- **B — Fail.** An unrequested foreground survivor dominates the left side and obscures a substantial part of the doors, undermining the requested girl-focused waist-up shot. The girl's hand appears on the viewer-facing side of the bar and the slightly open leaf's relationship to her and its reflections is ambiguous; her placement behind the glass does not read unambiguously. The graphic style alone did not prevent this glass/door spatial problem.
- **C — Pass, with a caveat.** The green sign is legible, the girl has two legs and grounded feet, and the door scale, converging corridor and floor reflections read coherently at phone size. Her feet meet the dark threshold, so exact depth behind the glass is suggested rather than independently provable.

**Result: 2/3 usable on Pass 1; the 3/3 target was not met.** No revisions were attempted. The owner has not reviewed these images.
