# Attempt 06 image generation record

All selected images were generated with the built-in image-generation tool; the model version was not exposed. These are new drawings for this attempt. The selected [character sheet](../../../../public/assets/001-kemacetan-terakhir/attempt-06/character-sheet.png) was used as the visual and identity reference for the four-panel atlases. The first proposed sheet was rejected because its facial design read too masculine; it was not used. The panels are extracted by [extract-panels.py](../extract-panels.py), without drawing new content. Usage and release rights need owner confirmation.

## Character sheet prompt

> Use case illustration-story. Production character sheet for a vertical serious Indonesian survival horror motion comic. Four storyboard panels, one consistent adult protagonist whose gender presentation is genuinely ambiguous, neither masculine-coded nor feminine-coded: rounded soft oval face and jaw, no facial hair, medium tan skin, medium-length tousled dark hair around cheeks and ears, slender but not muscular frame, plain olive utility jacket over charcoal shirt. Expressive realistic human eyes. Gritty dark-shaded inked anime/comic illustration, painterly shadows, desaturated blue-gray with muted red practical lights. Panel 1: front head and shoulders neutral. Panel 2: worried three-quarter look. Panel 3: seated inside driver's seat, collar, short natural neck, shoulders, torso and seat contact visible together. Panel 4: same seated person retracts their hand from a door handle, readable expression and arm. Consistent face, hair, body, outfit, and lighting; all four views same artist. No facial hair, no strong masculine jawline, no chibi, no text or watermark.

## Atlas prompts

The first four atlas prompts shared this prefix:

> Use case: illustration-story. Input image is the protagonist reference and visual-style guide: exactly match their face, gender-ambiguous presentation, medium tousled dark hair, tan skin, olive jacket, charcoal shirt, and gritty dark-shaded inked anime/comic illustration. Create a single clean 2-by-2 storyboard atlas with four separate distinct full-bleed image panels, each vertically composed for a 9:16 motion comic shot; straight grid dividing panels, no text, no speech balloons, no watermark. Consistent night car interior and same character throughout. Blue-black shadows, restrained red highlights.

- [Opening](atlas-opening.png): Four panels in order: 1 protagonist tense in driver's seat in foreground with a threatening infected silhouette looming outside side window, 2 protagonist looking ahead at packed standstill traffic through windshield, 3 protagonist flinching while reaching toward car door handle with danger beyond the glass, 4 protagonist withdrawing their hand from the handle and pressing door lock, expression tense. Show readable face and natural neck/shoulder/seat contact in each.
- [Lock and hiding](atlas-lock-hide.png): Four panels in order: 1 close POV of olive-sleeved hand beside a car door lock and silhouette beyond the rain-streaked window, 2 protagonist startled seeing someone attacked between packed cars outside, 3 protagonist using the ignition with worried expression, 4 protagonist ducks low below the side-window line with face visible and window frame above their head. Match car geography and reference character.
- [Night](atlas-night.png): Four panels in order: 1 protagonist crouched low and watching an infected person's shadow pass a side window, 2 close detailed view of threatening boots just outside the car door at night with window and door context, 3 protagonist sits tense and holds a half-full water bottle and a phone in the car, 4 protagonist watches the silent rain-streaked window at 3 am, fear visible in their face. Maintain consistent face and jacket.
- [Reveal](atlas-reveal.png): Four panels in order: 1 close-up empty rain-streaked side glass with no figure outside, 2 a distinct wider side window view from driver's seat with wet outside empty and dark, 3 protagonist recoils in the driver's seat looking back after a knock behind them, their face and upper body visible, 4 terrifying infected passenger sitting in rear seat of the same car behind the protagonist, revealed in dark red light, grounded anatomy, no gore. Maintain character and car continuity.
