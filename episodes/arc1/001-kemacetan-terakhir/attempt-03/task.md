## First sequence to build

1. Work on the few seconds after the second tap. Frame Damar from the chest up with the side window, jacket collar and headrest visible. Draw a small set of consistent views under the same strip of window light. Choose a recognizable hair part, jaw shape and collar seam. Compare the height of the skull and the position of the neck in each drawing before animating. Original illustrated raster poses or well-drawn vector poses are both workable.
2. Start with his attention outside. His eyes leave the window, his chin follows, the near cheek begins to hide the far eye, and the weight shifts into one shoulder against the seat. The jacket folds change with the shoulder. Use the actual angles the camera would see, including a passing three-quarter view as appropriate. Keep the headrest fixed so the rotation is legible. Give his final eyeline a specific destination in the next shot.
3. Inspect the two frames either side of the largest pose change. Look for cheeks swapping sides, duplicated eyes during blends, a collapsing skull, sliding neck, or a shadow that flips across the face. Play the passage at normal speed at 270 × 480 with captions hidden. If the drawing cannot sustain a wide continuous turn, stage a smaller visible turn and cut on the shoulder movement to a separately drawn rear three-quarter angle. Preserve the direction of the gaze across that cut.
4. Keep a short repeatable Studio playback interval and a labeled strip of the adjoining poses for the handoff. If the flat flip is still visible, revise this sequence before extending the approach.

## Carry the performance through the episode

- **Door decision.** Keep the boxed-in red sedan. Show what Damar sees through the side glass while his fingers are still on the handle. Make the runner or pursuer cross a visible part of the window before the voice confirms Damar’s choice. Pull fingers, wrist and elbow back as a connected gesture. Press the pin and let the hand withdraw so its down position is readable.
- **Hiding and relief.** Use the seat back and sill as fixed landmarks. Fold his upper body toward his lap as the shadow passes. As he rises, his eyes check the glass before his shoulders relax. Keep the half bottle, single pack of gum and 60% phone legible.
- **Passenger.** Give the tapping finger a clear contact point on the inside of the headrest. Show enough wrist and sleeve to connect it to the passenger. Frame the shoulder and face in the same cabin. If retiming the disclosure, align the first explicit “kursi belakang” with the pictured discovery and carry its captions with the spoken phrase. Record any editorial timing changes alongside the motion changes.
