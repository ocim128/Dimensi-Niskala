# Kemacetan Terakhir · attempt 04 remake · 19 November 2026

**Status: editable draft for Studio review.** A more dramatic viewer-facing cartoon remake with an original low drone, impact, footsteps, switches, taps and a reveal sting. It uses only the owner's specified recording for voice. The precise mix, speech and caption alignment require listening; the cartoon acting and depiction of the attack require further creative review.

## Checks and limits

- `npm run lint` and `npm run build` passed after the final composition changes. Studio opened and playback progressed through the timeline; no console errors or warnings were reported during the spot check. Thirteen stills were rendered and examined at 270 × 480. Still images do not prove smooth performance.
- Original narration `ffmpeg volumedetect`: mean −9.9 dBFS, max approximately 0 dBFS. Playback was lowered for headroom. There is no measured peak of the complete mixed composition and no subjective listening check; levels, intelligibility, pronunciation and caption sync remain open.
- The opening and final face are visible in sampled frames. The lunging figures are abstract and may not clearly read as an attack; the locking hand and the shadow could use a more literal action. The first clear rear-seat disclosure is the face at 76.17 s. The pre-reveal curved hand implies a presence behind the driver but its contact with the headrest is not clearly depicted.
- Platform overlays, first-viewer comprehension, continuous audiovisual review, and owner feedback remain unverified. Narration, music and effects rights are not cleared for publication. See [review](review.md).
