# Attempt 02 audit · Kemacetan Terakhir

**Decision on 18 November 2026.** Production and export are complete. The owner called the content “still bad but it was slightly better than first attempt”. Keep v2 as the comparison cut. The owner’s overall verdict is direct feedback. The diagnoses below are the reviewer’s reading of the cut.

**Owner’s priority: drawing and character movement.** Their most noticeable problem was that the head looked like a flat drawing flipping. The export shows exactly where it happens. At frame 1951 (65.033 s) Damar faces left. At 1952 (65.067 s) his nose, ear, highlights and hair silhouette all swap sides together. In the parent project, `src/KemacetanTerakhirV2/art.tsx` narrows the head drawing, then switches its horizontal scale from positive to negative when the turn passes 0.58. The shoulders retain essentially the same drawing. Slowing this animation would retain the one-frame reversal. [Six cropped frames, including the adjacent pair](head-turn-frames.jpg)

**Priority for attempt 3.** Test whether Damar looks like the same solid person turning in his seat. Redraw the intermediate views and carry the rotation into his collar and near shoulder. Preserve the voice and the surrounding story for the first comparison. The editing observations below are secondary to the owner’s stated concern. [Independent producer task](../attempt-03/task.md)

**Where the motion loses force.** A frame-change scan of the exported picture flagged the profile from 12.20 to 17.43 s as nearly still. In that shot the runner is positioned beyond the visible window and travels farther out. Damar blinks, but his pose offers little evidence that he has seen anything. The later duck-and-shadow shot demonstrates a more convincing way to stage danger using the same character and cabin. A hold works when the viewer has something specific to watch for.

**Evidence and limits.** I compared the [v1 sampled frames](../preview-contact.jpg) with the [v2 phone-size frames](review-frames.jpg), inspected [18 additional frames from the MP4](sequence-review.jpg), and checked the caption, movement and sound cue timings against the source. The frame-change scan used the upper 270 × 370 pixels of a 270 × 480 downscale to exclude captions, with an FFmpeg freeze threshold of −48 dB. It can miss small movements. The [export handoff](handoff.md) records stream and narration checks. Full listening, in particular the inventory line, and a first-time viewer's account remain pending. The Studio track overlap reported after delivery was fixed and the preview subsequently looped in Chrome.
