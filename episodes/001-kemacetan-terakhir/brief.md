# Kemacetan Terakhir

**Episode 001 · Motion-comic cut · 19 November 2026**

## Intent and continuity

The viewer is the driver of a maroon sedan in a fictional outbreak on an Indonesian toll road. Gridlock traps him. He sees an infected person tackle a rider, locks the door and hides. After the window falls quiet, a second knock comes from behind him. Whatever was outside, the threat is now in the back seat. The draft preserves the supplied second-person Indonesian narration, with room for the final image after the last line. The story stands alone.

The intended surprise depends on three images reading in order at phone size. The side window is empty, a pale hand rests on the driver’s headrest, and the infected passenger sits immediately behind him. Test whether a first-time viewer can describe where the danger is and when they discovered it.
