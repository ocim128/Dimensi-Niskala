## ImageGen prompt set

**Base direction for the first four atlases**

> Use case: illustration-story. Create a production art atlas for an original Indonesian motion-comic horror short. The attached image is the model sheet for the protagonist: preserve the round tan head, simple dot eyes, broad dark-brown swoop, olive jacket and ivory crew neck, short natural neck. Bold simple shapes, theatrical hand-painted backgrounds and rich screen-printed shadows, visible at phone size. Authentic Indonesian left-hand-traffic tollway with right-hand-drive maroon compact sedan. No gore. EXACT full-bleed 2 by 2 grid, perfectly straight quadrant divisions at exactly 50% width and 50% height, genuinely separate illustrations in each quadrant, zero borders or gutters; each is extracted into its own vertical shot. Tall portrait overall 9:16 if possible, maximum detail and size; place main subjects in the middle 80 percent width and around 35–55 percent of each panel vertically. Keep bottom 28 percent of each quiet and dark for captions. No typography, panel numbers, watermarks, or speech bubbles.
