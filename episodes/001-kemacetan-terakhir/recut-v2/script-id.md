# Script and caption source

The only spoken source is the supplied [original script](../script-id.md) and recording. The new audio is a 1.2× time-stretched derivative with no added voice or rewritten words. The numbered callouts express the lock and hide choices visually; their numbers are not spoken. The `script` object in [manifest.json](manifest.json) describes the intended dramatic anchors, not a claim that all the ticket's requested wording was recorded.

[captions.json](captions.json) contains the provisional four-word-maximum word-tagged beats. Timings were retimed from the earlier automatic alignment; long phrases were split proportionally, not word-aligned by listening. Naturalness and exact synchronization require a listener to review the full new mix.
