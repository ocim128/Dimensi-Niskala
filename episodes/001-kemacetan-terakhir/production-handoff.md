Scene boundaries are frame-exact in `timeline.json`. The captions are in `@remotion/captions` JSON shape and their start/end timestamps are **estimated by character proportion within each actual WAV**, not forced alignment. They require a human timing and listening pass before publishing. Scene, sound and captions can be edited in source; scenes are separate named timeline sequences. The repeated SVG art is generated inside the scene component, so its individual shapes are source-editable but not all individually selectable in Studio. Captions are JSON-driven rather than editable in Studio's caption editor.

## Generation and rights

All illustration and layout are original geometric SVG/React markup made for this preview. No third-party footage, stock audio, source/reference video, or source frames were used. The bed, clicks, footsteps, taps and reveal accent were generated from oscillators and seeded noise in `brief/build-timeline.py`. It is not a separately licensed music track. System Arial is used; the font itself is not bundled. The reference WAV was supplied in the brief as a synthetic voice reference.

Voice was generated from that WAV using Pocket TTS 3.1.0, pinned Indonesian six-layer config `anak10thn/pocket-tts-indonesian/indonesian_6l.yaml@17257664e384561c957b02ac92edd1a24807f0e5`; the config pins model weights at `a0cd8c1f1851fa32e5b31a948fa130193b125670` and tokenizer at `6a373f5f19c64f13adf7b813ffc6352bc7be944d`. Settings: CPU, default config temperature 0.3, sampler decode steps 1, EOS threshold -6.0, 24 kHz mono. Seed was not fixed, so regenerated audio will differ. Model/code use is subject to their own license terms, which have not been independently checked for the eventual use. Audio segments are retained for consistent review.

The original script was mostly retained. Inventory was naturally rephrased to: “Ia menghitung persediaannya. Setengah botol air. Sebungkus permen karet. Baterai ponselnya enam puluh persen.” Lines 05, 06, 07 and 11 were regenerated in short phrases after an independent Indonesian Whisper-small pass flagged possible missing or garbled words. A subsequent pass recognized the critical half-bottle and 60% details and the ending of 06; “permen karet” remained imperfectly recognized. ASR is not a listening review and does not prove every word or inflection.

## Checks and limitations

- `npm run build`: passed.
- `npm run lint`: passed after the production parent restored the existing ESLint config omitted from the isolated copy. Initial attempt failed before linting due to that missing config.
- `npx tsc --noEmit`: passed.
- Still frames for opening, inventory and reveal were rendered and visually inspected. All three inventory labels including 60% were seen. The opening was adjusted to keep the red car above the caption area.
- Full continuous playback, phone-size review, auditory intelligibility, detailed audio mix, caption alignment, and precise tap synchronization have **not** been checked by listening. This remains a review preview, not a claim that all review gates have passed.
