# Production brief — Kemacetan Terakhir

**Purpose:** Build an Indonesian vertical animated survival-thriller preview for a production and review test. This is fiction, not emergency guidance. The goal is an immersive story that can be understood on a phone without prior context.

## Independent production boundary

Work from this brief, the provided project and the narrator reference only. Do not search for, open, download, inspect, or reuse any source/reference video, its frames, audio, subtitles, transcript, repository history, or materials outside the supplied working project. The reviewer will handle comparison separately. Create original artwork, music and sound effects, or use assets with documented rights. Do not reuse third-party footage. Record what you actually used.

## Final experience

A lone driver named **Damar** is trapped in a gridlocked highway as a fictional zombie outbreak unfolds around him. People abandon their cars and run; Damar chooses to stay hidden in his own stationary car. He checks what he has left and believes he can wait for daylight. After the noise outside fades, a slow tapping comes from inside his car. He turns and discovers that an infected passenger has been in the back seat. End on that reveal, with an optional brief continuation card. The immediate action—choosing to hide—has a visible consequence, even though Damar's ultimate fate is unresolved.

The narrator describes what Damar does and believes, not what viewers should do in a real disaster. A car is **not** presented as a guaranteed safe place. Avoid gore and emergency instructions. The emotional arc is curiosity → panic outside → tense stillness → false relief → surprise.

## Format and delivery

- Vertical 1080 × 1920, 30 fps. Target roughly 65–75 seconds, adjusting the final duration to intelligible narration and natural pauses; timecodes below are planning estimates, not a reason to cut speech.
- Use an editable Remotion composition with a unique ID, frame-driven animation, independently selectable scene/overlay layers where practical, and locally available assets. Prepare an interactive Studio preview; do not export MP4 unless the owner explicitly asks.
- Produce the composition and source in the supplied isolated project. Save a clear handoff with the composition ID, exact changed/new source and asset paths, how to preview, actual duration, checks performed, known issues, and source/rights information. Do not publish or modify any account.
- Use the supplied selected v4 Indonesian male narrator reference and generation instructions. Preserve a clean narration track and editable script/caption source. Generate in short phrases if needed; inspect each phrase for missing words and awkward pacing, and document any deviations. The selected voice is a synthetic reference, not a real person's recording.

## Visual system

Create an original, coherent stylized 2D illustrated world with rounded geometric forms and strong silhouettes. Damar: adult man, short dark hair, green jacket over a light shirt, worried but readable face; keep his appearance consistent. His car: red compact sedan; clearly distinguish its front seat, rear seat, windows, door and dashboard. Infected figures: recognizable as a fictional threat with pale/desaturated skin and restrained horror, no graphic injury.

At dusk, use a purple-to-amber sky, muted charcoal highway, blocks of traffic and a smoky distant skyline. As night falls, transition toward navy, slate and low amber highlights. Emphasize danger sparingly with red. Use closeups for decisions and expression, wide shots for geography, and clean diagrams for locks, silence and inventory. Alternate scale and viewpoint so the video does not become a sequence of static title cards. Diagrams are integrated narrative moments, not a replacement for characters and action.

Maintain readable layout in 9:16: keep important faces, labels and captions away from screen edges and likely app overlays. Caption text should be high contrast, large and legible on a phone, phrase-timed to speech, with restrained yellow or red emphasis. Do not place essential visual details beneath captions. Limit shots and movements to what can be verified within the available time; avoid flashing, confusing teleportation, visual inconsistency and unnecessary clutter.

## Sound and captions

One consistent Indonesian male voice throughout, matching the supplied v4 reference and the owner's requested mature, firm delivery. Place narration clearly above an original sparse tension bed. Begin with subdued traffic and distant crowd ambience, then reduce the texture as the engine and radio switch off. Use deliberate silence before the taps and a brief restrained reveal accent. Avoid borrowed music, sirens so loud that speech is masked, gratuitous jump-scare volume, and unsynchronized sounds.

Break captions into short natural phrases that follow the actual final narration rather than displaying entire paragraphs. Make labels and captions editable. Use your own Indonesian text; no English narration. Track all music, sound effects, fonts, generated illustrations and voice assets in the handoff, with source/license status.

## Review gates

Before handing off, play the whole preview and check: opening hook; actions, geography and character consistency; readable subtitles and labels at phone size; correct inventory; intelligible narration; visual/audio timing; final reveal and end frame. Run `npm run lint` and `npm run build`, report real results, and distinguish any pre-existing failures. If you cannot verify playback or audio, mark that explicitly. No score or performance metrics should be fabricated.
