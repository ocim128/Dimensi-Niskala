# Episode 005 · Image generation prompts and handoff

## Set & Prop Anchors

ALLEY AND HOUSE ANCHOR: A straight, narrow, wet gray-concrete Indonesian residential gang runs toward a main road and the pedestrian overpass stairs. Looking toward the stairs, an unpainted concrete boundary wall is on the left and a locked modest house is on the right. The house has one closed dark-brown wooden single door, left hinges, a small silver lever at right, and a closed charcoal-gray iron gate with straight vertical bars. Beside the door is a rectangular window with fixed black vertical teralis bars, cream curtains and a reflective glass pane behind the bars. Overhead utility cables cross the alley. Cloudy 14:00 daylight is neutral cool gray. The woman's beige cloth tote contains a water bottle with a blue cap and a small black flashlight.

JPO AND GLASS BOOTH ANCHOR: At the main road end of the same wet gray-concrete gang, a small guard booth adjoins the foot of a gray concrete pedestrian overpass staircase with simple dark steel railings. Its exterior wall facing the road has a single large flat, intact reflective clear glass pane in a dark charcoal aluminum frame, with no horizontal bar across the middle; it is a wall panel, not a door. The woman stands outside close to this same pane, which is behind her shoulder; the pane faces her back and reflects the space immediately behind her. Cloudy 14:00 daylight is neutral cool gray. Her beige cloth tote contains a water bottle with a blue cap and a small black flashlight; her phone is a plain black smartphone.

These are production design choices for details not specified in the brief, not new story canon. The anchors were locked before generating panel 01. The exact applicable anchor is repeated verbatim in each prompt below.
