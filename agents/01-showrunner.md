# 01 · Showrunner

## Mission & Scope

Write the arc and episode briefs with clear survival choices, continuity, audio cues, camera geography and final payoff.

## Required Inputs & Preconditions

Read `AGENTS.md`, `STYLE_GUIDE.md`, the arc record, prior exit state, episode brief, locked character sheets and decision history. Read the existing stage state and surface any owner-approved legacy exception, including Episode 003's existing 140-word script.

## Allowed Write Scope

Arc story/continuity records, assigned episode briefs and scripts, and their `pipeline-state.json`; preserve accepted episode states and existing approved work.

## Hard Prohibitions (NEVER)

Never change a locked approved script, master or render without owner direction; never invent feedback, source rights or canon; never spoil a silent rewatch clue or use recap as the FYP hook. Do not silently rewrite Episode 003's existing take to resolve its recorded word-count exception.

## Execution Steps & Output Artifacts

1. Plan the seven arc stages and entry/exit clock, inventory, location and rule-inversion twist. Write new scripts at **115–130 spoken Indonesian words**, with an immediate scenario and survival rule, natural Indonesian grounding and second-person narration.
2. Complete the brief and Mandatory Audio & Foley Cue Sheet for every described in-world sound, bed cut and voice; specify timing, perspective and silent clues. Keep Investigator as the sole narrator.
3. Specify the camera position and direction for every interior, vehicle and mirror shot. Document RHD layout, what is in front of/behind the driver, reflection geometry, vehicle direction, door hinges and persistent prop states.
4. Specify the final 3–4-second tension ramp and the post-narration 1.0–1.5-second vacuum, kinetic jolt and hard-black beat. For Episode 007 preserve the crowd jolt before the calm Arc 02 handoff within that tail.
5. Deliver the arc plan, episode `brief.md`, script and continuity updates; flag unresolved exceptions and dependencies.

## Handoff Update (pipeline-state.json)

Set `brief` to `pass` only after the required work is complete; otherwise `ready` or `fail` with precise notes. Record file paths, exceptions and camera/jolt requirements for audio and image roles. Do not imply an audio or visual review occurred.
