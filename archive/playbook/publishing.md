# Publishing and packaging

Publish only when the owner has authorized posting and the episode has passed review. A production request alone does not authorize posting, scheduling, messaging or changing an account. If account access, a platform action or a required asset right is unavailable, report the limitation instead of claiming publication.

## Before upload

- Confirm the final export is the intended version, plays correctly with audio, and remains understandable on a phone. Review the actual TikTok and YouTube upload previews for UI overlap, cropping and caption legibility.
- Prepare an Indonesian title/hook, a concise description that sets expectations, and relevant tags where useful. Each should honestly reflect the episode. A platform-specific version is fine, but log material changes so later comparisons are interpretable. Avoid claims about guaranteed reach, arbitrary hashtag counts or a fixed posting time.
- Check the asset ledger for commercial/platform usage rights, attribution obligations and any voice likeness permission. Do not re-upload the reference video or use unlicensed source material.
- Check current platform rules on AI-generated or altered content for the specific imagery and audio. Disclose when required, using the applicable platform controls. Do not assume a generic “AI” tag alone satisfies either platform. Official sources: [YouTube AI disclosure](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491) and [TikTok AI-generated content](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content). Checked 2026-11-18; recheck when publishing because policies and interfaces change.
- Review current applicable community and copyright policies. Mark fictional events clearly where realistic presentation might mislead; do not present invented survival methods as dependable real-world advice.

## Record after publication

For each platform record the episode ID, published URL, date/time and timezone, title, description, tags, disclosed AI status, actual version and material differences. Capture metrics at the planned observation times in a copy of [the performance template](../../templates/performance.md). Record when figures were observed, not just their values. Use the platform's own metric labels and definitions; unavailable metrics stay blank or `not available`, never zero by assumption. Keep platform data separate.

Official analytics starting points: [YouTube Shorts analytics](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12942217?co=YOUTUBE._YTVideoType%3Dshorts&hl=en-GB) and [TikTok comment insights](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/growing-your-audience/comment-insights-on-tiktok). Availability and definitions may differ by account and change over time.
